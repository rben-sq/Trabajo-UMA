A stew of something more beef than mutton, salpicón most nights, duels and misfortunes on Saturdays, lantejas on Fridays, and a few pigeons on Sundays, consumed three parts of his estate.
The rest consisted of a night watchman's coat, velvet breeches for festivals, with his slippers of the same quality, and on weekdays he honored himself with his finest velvet robe.
He had in his house a housekeeper who was over forty, a niece who was not yet twenty, and a farmhand and stable boy who saddled the horse as well as he wielded the pruning shears. 
Our gentleman's age was approaching fifty; He was of robust build, lean of flesh, lean of face, an early riser, and a fan of the hunt.
